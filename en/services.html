What we treat

From a routine check-up to a full set of implants. All in one clinic, with doctors who know your history.

Dental implants

Implants replace missing teeth, from one tooth to a full arch. Implant surgery is done by Dr. Yurii Prydatko.

  • A single implant for one missing tooth
  • Several implants when a few teeth in a row are missing
  • A full arch on six implants (All-on-6) when few teeth remain
  • Bone grafting and sinus lift when there is not enough bone

Aesthetics and whitening

Whiter teeth, an even colour, new crowns instead of old ones. We match everything so the result looks natural.

  • Magic Smile whitening in one visit
  • Veneers to change the shape and colour of front teeth
  • Zirconia crowns to replace old crowns
  • Aesthetic restorations: old fillings replaced with invisible ones

Fillings and root canals

We treat cavities and inflamed nerves, place fillings and rebuild broken teeth. First an examination and a plan, then treatment.

  • Examination and treatment plan at your first visit
  • Cavity treatment and fillings
  • Root canal treatment to save the tooth
  • Rebuilding a broken tooth
  • Extractions when a tooth cannot be saved

Orthodontics

We straighten teeth and correct bites for children and adults. Treatment is led by orthodontist Dr. Iryna Hlova.

  • Braces for children and adults
  • Aligners, clear trays instead of braces
  • Early treatment for children while the bite is still forming
  • Retainers to keep teeth straight after treatment
Book with the orthodontist

Children’s dentistry

We treat children from age three. We take our time so the child gets used to the doctor and comes back without fear.

  • A first check-up and a look around the room
  • Treating baby teeth
  • Fissure sealants and fluoride against cavities
  • Brushing lessons as a game

Professional hygiene

Regular cleaning keeps teeth and gums healthy and catches problems early. We recommend coming every six months.

  • Ultrasonic cleaning to remove tartar
  • Stain and plaque removal
  • Polishing and fluoride
  • Gum care and treating inflammation
